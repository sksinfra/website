Who we are

We are an infrastructure company built around one discipline: draw the line well, then build it exactly. From national expressways to electrified rail corridors, from water mains to power networks, SKS Infra Projects takes public infrastructure from alignment to asset.

Headquartered in Agra with a corporate office in Noida, our teams are on site across India's road, rail and power corridors, working for the authorities that plan the nation's growth.

  • Engineering first

    Design, planning and method are settled before the first machine moves, so what gets built is what was promised.

  • One accountable team

    Civil, structural, electrical and systems work run to a single programme, with nothing lost at the hand-offs.

  • Built to be relied on

    Public infrastructure is judged over decades. We build, test and hand over with that horizon in mind.

How we deliver

Every contract model.
One standard of delivery.

Public infrastructure is procured in different ways. We are structured to deliver under each of them, taking on as much of the asset's life as the project calls for.

A finished asset for a fixed price.

One lump-sum contract for the complete facility. We carry design, procurement and construction, and hand over infrastructure that is ready to open, then stand behind it through the maintenance period.

Our scopeDesign · Procurement · Construction · Maintenance period

Shared funding. Long-term accountability.

A public-private partnership in which construction is part-funded by the authority and part by us. We then operate and maintain the road through the concession and are paid in annuities, so quality is our long-term interest too.

Our scopeDesign · Part-finance · Construction · Operation and maintenance

We finance it, build it, run it and hand it back.

We raise the capital, build the asset and operate it across the concession period before transferring it to the authority in the condition the agreement requires.

Our scopeDesign · Finance · Construction · Operation and maintenance · Transfer

The authority's design, executed precisely.

Works measured and paid at agreed unit rates against the client's drawings and bill of quantities. For projects where the scope is defined and disciplined execution is what matters.
