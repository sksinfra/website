01Who we are

SKS Infra Projects Pvt. Ltd. (“SKS”, “we”, “us”) is an infrastructure company with its head office at 21, Bagh Farzana, Civil Line, Agra – 282002, Uttar Pradesh, India.

We decide why and how the personal data described in this policy is used. That makes us the “Data Fiduciary” for it under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You can reach us at info@sksinfraprojects.com.

02What this policy covers

This policy explains how we handle personal data that reaches us through:

  • our website, sksinfraprojects.com;
  • emails you send us, including job applications;
  • the tools we use to publish and manage our official LinkedIn Page, including our LinkedIn developer application.

It does not cover websites or services run by others that we link to, such as LinkedIn itself. Those have their own privacy policies.

03Personal data we collect

  • Enquiries. When you contact us through the “Get in touch” form or by email: your name, your email address, your message and anything else you choose to include, such as your organisation, phone number or attachments.
  • Job applications. When you send us your CV: the details in your CV and covering email, such as your education, work history and contact details.
  • Technical data. Our website hosting provider may automatically record basic technical data, such as IP address, browser type and the date and time of a visit, for security and to keep the site running. We do not use this data to identify you.
  • LinkedIn Page data. See section 4.

We do not ask for sensitive information through our website. Please do not send us financial account details, government ID numbers or health information unless we have specifically asked for them for a stated purpose.

04Our LinkedIn Page tools

We use an internal application to publish and schedule posts on our own LinkedIn Page, the SKS Infra Projects Page, and to see how those posts perform. Only SKS uses the application. It is not offered to the public or to other businesses.

When an authorised administrator of our Page signs in through LinkedIn, the application may access:

  • the administrator's basic LinkedIn profile details (name and LinkedIn member ID) and an access token, used only to confirm their authority to act for our Page;
  • information about our Page, and the posts, images and documents we publish on it;
  • reactions, comments and aggregate statistics on our own posts, used only to understand how our content performs and to respond to comments.

We use LinkedIn data only for these purposes. We do not sell it, use it for advertising or profiling, combine it with other data to identify people, display it outside LinkedIn, or use it to train artificial-intelligence models.

We follow LinkedIn's API terms and data storage requirements. Member profile data and social activity data received through LinkedIn's APIs are not stored for longer than LinkedIn permits, currently 24 hours and 48 hours respectively. Statistics about our own Page and posts that do not identify anyone may be kept for longer, but never beyond the limit LinkedIn sets for Page data, currently one year.

We use trusted service providers to run this application, such as an integration platform that connects securely to LinkedIn and an AI writing assistant that helps us draft content. They process data only on our instructions and only to run the application.

05How we use personal data

  • To reply to your enquiry and continue any conversation you start with us.
  • To assess job applications and contact candidates.
  • To publish and manage content on our LinkedIn Page, as described in section 4.
  • To keep our website and systems secure and working.
  • To meet our legal obligations and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We handle enquiries and job applications because you have chosen to send them to us and, where we ask for it, on the basis of your consent. We use them only for the purpose you sent them for. We do not use your data for direct marketing without your separate consent.

06Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only:

  • with service providers who host our website, process form submissions, provide email, or run the tools described in section 4, under confidentiality and data-protection obligations;
  • within SKS, with the people who need it to handle your enquiry or application;
  • with government authorities, courts or regulators when the law requires it.

Some of our service providers may store or process data outside India. Where they do, we take reasonable steps to ensure your data stays protected as Indian law requires.

07How long we keep it

  • Enquiries: up to 2 years after our last communication with you.
  • Job applications: up to 12 months, unless you join SKS or ask us to keep your CV on file for longer.
  • LinkedIn data: as set out in section 4.
  • Technical logs: held by our hosting provider for as long as it needs them for security, under its own retention practices.

We keep data for longer only when the law requires it or when it is needed for a legal claim. After that, we delete it or make it anonymous.

08How we protect it

We use reasonable security safeguards to protect personal data from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. These include encrypted (HTTPS) connections, access limited to authorised staff, and secure sign-in through LinkedIn for our Page tools.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

09Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, how we use it and who we have shared it with;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update your data;
  • ask us to erase your data when it is no longer needed for the purpose you shared it for, unless the law requires us to keep it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. This does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated;
  • raise a grievance with us (section 11) and, if it is not resolved, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@sksinfraprojects.com. We may need to verify your identity before we act.

10Cookies and children

Our website does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Our website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.
